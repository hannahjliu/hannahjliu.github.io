About the Data

▼

The ICE detention data were obtained through a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request by the Deportation Data Project. This tool filters for detentions of individuals under 18 years of age. Records are deduplicated on unique identifier and book-in date.

Some entries list multiple facility codes separated by semicolons; selecting any one code will return all records containing it. Age is calculated from birth year and book-in date. Stay length is the difference between stay book-out and stay book-in dates. Where no book-out date or departure date exists, the child is assumed to still be detained, and stay length is calculated through March 11, 2026.

Raw data can be downloaded from deportationdata.org.
