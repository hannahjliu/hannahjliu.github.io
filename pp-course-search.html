    Scraped directly from all 87 individual LSE PP course guide pages (2026/27 session, School of Public Policy). Convenor, full course description, assessment breakdown, term(s) taught, unit value, suspended status, programme availability, and compulsory status all come straight from each course's own guide page (a course only counts as "compulsory" for a programme if its own Availability text literally says "compulsory on" that programme, not merely "available on"). Compulsory courses are pinned to the top of the list — for whichever programme(s) you've ticked, or for any tracked programme if none are ticked. The description is hidden under a "Course description" toggle on each card, but the search box always searches its full text — if your term only matches inside the description, that course's description auto-expands so you can see why it matched. Both MPA and MPA-DSPP are split into Year 1 and Year 2 based on each course's own wording and LSE's programme regulations. "Full year" means the course spans at least Autumn Term and Winter Term. Click "View course guide" for the full official page.
