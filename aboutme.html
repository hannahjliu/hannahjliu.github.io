About Me

Hello!

My name is Hannah Liu and I'm a Master of Public Administration student at the London School of Economics and Political Science.

Before London, I lived in Washington D.C. where I worked in federal policy research and advocacy to support immigrant communities. I served as the Manager of Immigration Advocacy at Asian Americans Advancing Justice | AAJC, a national Asian American civil rights organization. Prior to that, I was a Policy Analyst on the Immigration and Immigrant Families team at the Center for Law and Social Policy, an antipoverty nonprofit think tank.

I’m originally from Seattle, Washington, but I attended St. Olaf College in Minnesota where I majored in Political Science and Music. My many hobbies include fiber arts, pottery, reading, and most recently, cleaning and joining datasets at 3am.
